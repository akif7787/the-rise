✳ OUR EDITORIAL APPROACH

Great stories begin with good evidence.

The Rise is a Bangladesh-first magazine celebrating research, invention, awards, and thoughtful leadership. We want the real work behind an achievement to be as visible as the recognition.

Submit a Story Read Spotlights

FOUR PRINCIPLES

How we verify & publish.

Evidence before applause
01

Primary Evidence First

Editors review original research papers, official competition results, award certificates, institutional announcements, and live working platforms before publishing.

02

Clear Outcomes vs. Prototypes

Profiles distinguish verified outcomes, prizes, and published milestones from early ambitions and prototypes, noting important context and limitations.

03

Consent, Credit & Accuracy

We seek contributor consent, confirm exact names, affiliations, and quotations, credit supplied photographs honestly, and correct any factual errors openly.

04

Editorial Independence

Getting featured as an achiever is always free. Payment never buys an editorial spotlight; commercial sponsorships on our Advertise page are clearly separated and labelled.

TRANSPARENCY

Source & Profile Disclosures.

Community-submitted profiles: Profiles such as Mohammad Nasir Uddin use a supplied photograph and community-submitted achievement details; each story clearly notes its verification status and source context.

News-reported profiles: Other achiever spotlights are compiled from verified public news reports and institutional announcements, with the original publication and date credited directly inside each story.

Corrections & updates: If you spot a correction or want to update a published profile with additional official evidence, email hello@therisebd.com.
