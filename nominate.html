CHECKLIST

Before you submit

  • Include full name, address, email, and active WhatsApp number so editors can verify details quickly.
  • Add high-resolution photos with clear lighting for the featured spotlight card.
  • Upload crisp proof images (certificates, official result screenshots, award letters, or participation badges).
  • Every spotlight is reviewed against real proof before publication.
