OUR STORY

About The Rise

Real people. Real achievements.

The Rise is a Bangladesh-first magazine about people who do good work — researchers, inventors, award winners, athletes, artists and quiet changemakers. Every profile is based on a real achievement, with its source credited in the story.

Know someone doing great work? Submit a nomination — and share where the achievement can be verified.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything about getting featured
Who can submit a story or nominate someone?

Anyone! You can nominate a researcher, student, innovator, athlete, artist, or community leader—or submit your own verified achievement directly via our Submit a Nomination page.

Does it cost anything to be featured as an achiever?

No. Editorial spotlights on The Rise are 100% free and based purely on merit and verified proof. Brands looking to promote products or events can book banner slots on our Advertise page.

What proof or documents should I attach when submitting?

Upload clear image screenshots or photos of certificates, award letters, conference badges, competition results, or links to published papers/news reports so our editors can verify the milestone.

How can I contact The Rise directly?

Write to our official email hello@therisebd.com or message us on WhatsApp at +880 1643-442518.
